Long-Range Vision

From MVP to language-culture infrastructure.

Beyond the 12-month funding phase, our internal roadmap sketches four longer epochs. These are directional, not committed timelines, and are included here to give partners and prospective hires a sense of ambition and sequencing.

I

Foundation & Market Entry — Years 1–2

Core MVP modules (daily word discovery, spaced-repetition flashcards, visual progress tracking), first subscription tiers, and an early, engaged community.

II

Culture & Learning Platform — Years 2–3

An interactive map for language and cultural journeys, expanded learning games, a community marketplace, and adaptive AI-driven learning algorithms.

III

Premium, AI & Research — Years 3–4

Premium modules, a creator economy for original content, a live AI tutor for pronunciation and style, and deepened research partnerships.

IV

AI Infrastructure & Global Scale — Year 4+

Proprietary European compute infrastructure, an exportable platform model for additional languages, and Disce as an international center for language research.
