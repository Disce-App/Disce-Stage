The Problem

You have B2 or C1 — but something is still missing.

Many advanced learners understand German well. In the decisive moment, precision is what's missing: the right register, adequate politeness, the tone that actually lands.

01

Understanding without saying

In meetings, seminars, or appointments with authorities, learners think more than they actually say — comprehension has outpaced expression.

02

Uncertainty of tone

Does this sound too direct? Too soft? Too colloquial? Too formal? Register — not vocabulary — is where advanced learners stall.

03

Course progress ≠ life progress

Certificates accumulate, but presentations, applications, and everyday conversation don't get any easier. Fewer than 20% of B2 learners reach C1 without targeted intervention.

Who Disce Is For

Advanced learners who need German to work for them, not just be understood.

Disce serves international professionals, students, and residents at the B2–C2 level who want to use German confidently and effectively — not just comprehend it.

🎓

Academic

Students giving presentations, writing academic papers, and participating in seminars — where structured argumentation and precise terminology are the currency of credibility.

💼

Professional

Qualified white-collar professionals who need to persuade in meetings, draft correspondence, and negotiate — where register mismatch reads as a competence gap, fairly or not.

🌍

Everyday

People building a life in the DACH region who want full participation in civic and social life — not just comprehension, but a voice that lands the way they intend.

How Disce Works

A clear path, not a vocabulary list.

Set a goal, take a short benchmark, then train precisely what real communication demands of you — with feedback grounded in established learning psychology, not gamified point-chasing.

01

Choose a goal

The B2 exam, a presentation, a job application, everyday life in the DACH region — the learner defines what everything else is calibrated against.

02

A short benchmark

In 12–15 minutes, we establish where the learner stands across speaking, writing, listening, and reading — including register and prosody, not just vocabulary recall.

03

Daily, structured coaching

Short writing and speaking sessions with feedback on tone, typical phrasing, and communicative effect — plus a transparent, multidimensional progress profile.
